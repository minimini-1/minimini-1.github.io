## Abstract

Subject-driven image personalization generates new images that preserve the identity of one or several reference subjects in novel scenes. Existing generalized methods typically require large collections of paired reference and composed-target images produced through costly multi-stage curation pipelines. We introduce CRAFT (Constrained Reward via Attention Fine-Tuning), a single-step ReFL framework that fine-tunes a reference-aware multimodal diffusion transformer through LoRA adapters using only 10K reference images and subject masks, without composed-target supervision. CRAFT applies attention-level rewards to align noise and phrase-token attention with the correct reference subject, then uses the resulting per-subject attention masks to guide an identity reward. Applied to FLUX.2-klein-9B, CRAFT achieves state-of-the-art performance on XVerseBench while using substantially less training data than prior generalized approaches.
